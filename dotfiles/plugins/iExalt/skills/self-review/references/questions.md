# Question bank

Pick what the ledger leaves open. Skip any question the evidence, config or earlier answers settle,
and list the answer you assumed so the user can overturn it.

## Self

### Gaps in the evidence

- Which talks, demos or show-and-tells did you give? Where are the slides, and was it recorded?
- Has anyone praised your work out loud or in a channel the miners could not reach?
- What work is invisible when it works: plumbing, access, migrations, reliability?
- Which cross-team or cross-org work did you do, and who on the other side would vouch for it?
- Whom did you onboard, mentor or unblock?
- Is there a number you measured yourself that beats the one in the ledger?

### Your view

- Which frustration deserves its own heading, and why did it matter?
- For each challenge, what would you actually do differently? Skip the line if nothing honest fits.
- Which of your strengths should the review sell, and which example proves each one?
- What are your goals for the next period, in your words? Which is first?
- What do you need from your manager to reach them?

### Checks

- Confirm names the evidence may have confused (tools, projects, teams).
- Confirm who each quote was about.
- Confirm every period and move date.

## Peer

- How did you work with them, and when?
- What did they do that made a difference to you or your team? Which moment stuck with you?
- What should they keep doing? What could they do more of or differently, and what would change if
  they did?
- Is there anything you want to avoid saying, or anyone who will read this besides them and their
  manager?
- Confirm the facts the ledger is unsure of: dates, ownership, numbers.
